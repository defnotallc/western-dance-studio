Privacy Policy — Western Dance Studio

Last updated: April 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Western Dance Studio ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our iOS application.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Western Dance Studio does not require account creation and does not collect personally identifiable information (name, email, phone number) from you directly.

Location (Optional)

If you grant location permission, the App uses your device's location solely to show nearby country dance venues on the Venues map. Your location is used on-device only and is never transmitted to our servers or stored remotely. You can revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings.

Local Data

The App stores the following on your device only using iOS UserDefaults:

  • Your favorite dances list
  • Your onboarding completion state
  • Metronome preferences

This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob

Western Dance Studio displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use certain information to serve ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Identifier for Advertisers, if you permit tracking)
  • IP address and general location (country/region)
  • App usage data relevant to ad delivery

Google's use of information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can learn about opting out of personalized ads at Google Ad Settings.

YouTube

Dance instructional videos are embedded from YouTube via YouTubePlayerKit. When you play a video, YouTube (Google) may collect data per its Privacy Policy. The App does not transmit your identity to YouTube.

Apple MapKit

The Venues feature uses Apple MapKit to display maps and perform geocoding. Apple's privacy practices apply; see Apple's Privacy Policy.

App Tracking Transparency

If prompted, you may choose to allow or deny tracking by advertising networks. Choosing "Ask App Not to Track" limits ads to non-personalized delivery but does not eliminate ads. Your choice is respected regardless of the option selected.

Data Retention

Because we do not collect personal data, there is nothing for us to retain, export, or delete on our servers. Local data on your device can be cleared at any time by deleting the app.

Children's Privacy

Western Dance Studio is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information in some way, please contact us and we will promptly address the issue.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
